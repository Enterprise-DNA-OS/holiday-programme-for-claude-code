# Programme record checks

Source checked 4 October 2026: [Te Kāhui Kāhu, OSCAR Specialist Accreditation Standard, Level 3, February 2026](https://www.xn--tekhuikhu-7bbe.govt.nz/assets/accreditation-approval/specialist-standards/February-2026-versions/OSCAR-Specialist-Standard-Feb-2026.pdf). This build is for New Zealand holiday programme administration. Australian operators need their own rules reviewed and configured.

The checks flag missing evidence. Passing them does not establish accreditation, physical supervision, collector identity, staff suitability or complete legal compliance. They cover only the subset below. Follow the operator's safety procedures when a child is unaccounted for. No funding claim, payment or medication decision is made here.

| Rule | Record check | Source and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OSCAR-CONTACTS | Fewer than two named contacts | Criterion 2, page 4. Contact rows need a phone. Staff confirm these are distinct reachable people. |
| OSCAR-PICKUP | No authorised collector | Criteria 2 and 3, pages 4 and 5. Check-out accepts only a contact authorised for that child. The operator verifies identity and any restrictions. |
| OSCAR-MEDICAL | Missing recorded medical review or self-medication instructions and plan | Criterion 2, page 4, and criterion 6, page 8. No treatment advice is generated. |
| OSCAR-SAFETY-PLAN | Court order notes without a safety plan reference | Criterion 3, page 5. Preserve original documents separately and have staff interpret them. |
| OSCAR-ATTENDANCE | Finished session without collection or an explained absence | Criterion 3, page 5. Bookings never become attendance automatically. |
| OSCAR-INCIDENT | Missing incident details, treatment or parent acknowledgement | Criterion 4, page 6. Record verbal or written confirmation accurately. The system does not send or sign. |
| OSCAR-FIRST-AID | Upcoming booked session lacks a current rostered first aider | Criterion 1, page 3. Coverage assumes the rostered adult remains for the whole session. Staff verify presence and competence. |
| POLICY-RATIO | Confirmed bookings exceed roster count times the configured ratio | Criterion 1, pages 2 and 3. The standard recommends 1:10 on site and 1:8 off site, and permits justified alternatives. These are recommendations, not universal mandatory ratios. Set each session's approved ratio. The default 10 is only a starting configuration. |
| OSCAR-RAMS | Off-site session lacks a risk plan reference | Criterion 1, page 3. Significant on-site risks also need assessment. The software does not decide which activities present significant risk. |
| OSCAR-DRILL | An active or closed holiday programme has no drill within its dates | Criterion 5, page 7. During a running programme this is a reminder, not proof of a missed deadline. |
| LOCAL-STAFF-REVIEW | Missing or overdue staff review date | Local operator reminder only. No statutory safety-check interval is encoded or certified. |

The ten analysis questions also flag enrolment information not reviewed within 180 days. This is a local review interval, not a legal deadline. Change it to suit the operator's policy through a reviewed migration or query change.

Other gaps need human review: permissions to travel alone, actual signatures, medication administration logs, document retention, privacy access requests, policy availability and emergency actions. Unaccompanied departure is not supported by check-out. Configure it with verified consent and instructions before using it. Source references in results identify the standard, not an assertion that every finding is a breach.
